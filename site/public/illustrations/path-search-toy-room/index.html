    How to read this
    • The grid is a toy room of 16 by 9 subtiles drawn for this page. Dark cells are walls; the cells outside the room count as walls too.
    • Search: the walk runs depth first under a bound on the cost; if it finds no route the bound grows by 5 and it runs again over the same cell store.
    • Random walk and spiral: both ignore the grid. The walk keeps its first direction plus or minus one or straight; the spiral grows around the start.
    • Every value is computed by our model for the toy numbers given, not observed in the game.
