    How to read this
    • The toy room: the light cells are the logic box, the dark cells outside it are only reached by a ring walk, the bright blocks are walls the grid marks as blocked.
    • The leader: a random point in the box (two room draws), refused when it is too close to the warp point, then a probe and the exact placement, each with three more room draws.
    • Each member: a walk round square rings 3, 6 and 9 subtiles from the leader (radius 3), each ring with its own three room draws. It starts at a random cell of the ring, moves before it tests, skips a cell outside the box untested, and takes the first cell whose footprint is clear.
    • Every value is computed by our model for the illustrative room and seed, not observed in the game.
