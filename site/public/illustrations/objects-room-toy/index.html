Calculations for this step
    How to read this
    • The room seed S makes two draws for a passing slot and one for the rest: the slot roll, then the member pick. The object-region seed O makes every draw of the placement.
    • A slot passes when its roll is at most its chance; the pick adds the members' PROB values up and takes the first member whose running sum is above the second roll.
    • The handler turns the member's DENSITY into a count from the room's size and tries to place that many objects.
    • Each placement makes up to five attempts of two draws; an attempt is kept only inside the margin and with no marked cell in the box around it. There is no fallback.
    • Every number is an illustrative input or computed from one, not observed in the game.
