    How to read this
    • Step 2: every name of the seller's range gets a seed, one step of the store seed per name, in name order, before any pick.
    • Picks: ten draws over the range; a pick that lands on a name already listed moves on to the next free name, with no further draw. The grid has one cell per name, in range order.
