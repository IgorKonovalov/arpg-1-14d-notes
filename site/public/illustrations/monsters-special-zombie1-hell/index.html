    How to read this
    • Top: the mods of the unit so far, in the order they are written. A champion gets one mod and no more; a unique gets 1 + difficulty of them.
    • Champion chance: a draw from 0 to 99 below the chance of the first MonUMod row (20) makes a champion, and then no draw is made for the unique group.
    • A pick: the rows with a weight of the pick's kind are tested against the class and version; the ones left are the candidates, one draw from 0 to the total picks the first whose running weight passes it.
    • Only the winner's weight is shown, not the table. Every value is computed by our model for the illustrative inputs given, not observed in the game.
