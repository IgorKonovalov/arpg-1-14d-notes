Calculations for this step
    How to read this
    • The top row names the steps of one pick: the affix level (alvl), draw 1, the filter, the weights and draw 2.
    • Draw 1: the low bit of the first step of the item seed decides whether the pick rolls at all.
    • Filter: counts only, one line per test in the game's order, with the rows each test refused and the rows left. No row of the table is listed.
    • Weights and draw 2: the strip runs over the total weight in table order; the listed candidates and the winner are bars as wide as their weights, and each run of the other candidates is one hatched block. Draw 2 picks a point along it, and the candidate under the point wins. At most five candidates are listed, by affix id and weight.
    • Every value is computed by our model of the pick for the illustrative inputs given, not observed in the game.
