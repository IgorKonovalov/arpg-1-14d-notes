Calculations for this step
    How to read this
    • The strip is a row of subtiles; the unit starts at the centre of the first one and has one point, at the centre of a later one.
    • Each game frame the unit moves by a step computed from its path speed; the step is the same on every frame until the last one.
    • On the last frame the point is nearer than a whole step, so the step becomes exactly the distance left and the unit lands on the point.
    • Positions are in 1/65536 of a subtile. Every value is computed by our model for the toy numbers given, not observed in the game.
