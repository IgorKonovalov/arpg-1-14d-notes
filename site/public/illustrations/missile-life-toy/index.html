Calculations for this step
    How to read this
    • The toy missile is made with the creation rules of B1: its velocity, range, frames left and activate mark come from the row and the level.
    • Each frame the do function steps the velocity (every fifth step it adds the acceleration up to the cap), moves the position by the step and takes one off the frames left.
    • Only once the frames left reach the activate mark does the missile test its cell for a unit; a unit found there is hit.
    • The hit takes the owner's next draw as a to-hit roll; below the chance it is a hit, otherwise the missile is freed at that unit.
    • Every number is an illustrative input or computed from one, not observed in the game.
