    How to read this
    • Top row: the seed's two words before the draw, the multiplier, and the 64-bit result low x multiplier + high; its upper half becomes the new high word and its lower half the new low word.
    • Middle: the draw is the new low word mod n (here n is the range); that remainder is the number the game uses.
    • Bottom: the numbers drawn so far, newest highlighted. A range below 1 would draw nothing and leave the seed alone; it is not shown.
    • Every value is computed by our model of the generator for the illustrative seed given, not observed in the game.
